Nigeria's Dangote oil refinery closes 'historic' IPO

News · Africanews · 2026-10-28T12:13:52.000Z

Africa's largest ever initial public offering closed on Wednesday after raising funds from investors in 120 countries. The IPO for Nigeria's Dangote Petroleum Refinery was extended by two weeks to allow more Africans to participate.
