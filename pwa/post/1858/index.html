Self-sufficient mobile weather station

IoT & Open Source · Raspberry Pi · 2026-11-09T21:08:13.000Z

Jack Young designed a self-powered, modular Raspberry Pi Zero W weather station for hobbyists and researchers. It was tested on the Gower Peninsula in Wales, measuring temperature, humidity, wind speed, and light intensity while transmitting data to a web server.
