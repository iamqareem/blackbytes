Sudan: One year after the fall of El-Fasher

News · France24 Africa · 2026-10-27T11:46:35.000Z

It has been one year since the fall of El-Fasher in Sudan, a city in North Darfur, which was captured by the Rapid Support Forces. At least 6,000 civilians were killed by rebel troops as they entered this strategic city.
