Ukraine: massive fire engulfs buildings after Russian strike on Kharkiv

News · Africanews · 2026-10-28T07:43:30.000Z

Five people, including a 4-year-old child, were injured in an overnight Russian drone strike on Kharkiv. The attack set several buildings ablaze, with firefighters working under the threat of further strikes.
