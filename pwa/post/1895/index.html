The AI-Assisted Linux Driver for DisplayLink Gets Legal Notice

IoT & Open Source · It's FOSS · 2026-11-11T13:08:00.000Z

Synaptics has sent cease-and-desist letters to open-source developer Mike Lothian, demanding he stop development of Vino, an AI-assisted Linux driver for DisplayLink docks, alleging patent infringement and demanding $25,000 to stop weekly fee increases.
