Taiwo Awoniyi Honoured with ‘Beyond the Pitch’ Award at the 2026 Best of Africa Awards in London

Entertainment · BellaNaija · 2026-10-28T10:30:10.000Z

Nigerian striker Taiwo Awoniyi received the PFA Beyond the Pitch Award at the 2026 Best of Africa Awards in London, celebrating his Premier League career with Nottingham Forest and his community work in Nigeria, including funding the Kwara Kiddies League in Ilorin.
