Netanyahu sought private talks with Palestinian party leader despite public attacks

Geopolitics · Middle East Eye · 2026-11-11T13:07:18.000Z

Israeli caretaker PM Benjamin Netanyahu secretly met with United Arab List leader Mansour Abbas despite publicly calling Palestinian parties "terror supporters," putting Abbas at risk. The talks centered on coalition dynamics ahead of the new parliament's opening in Jerusalem.
