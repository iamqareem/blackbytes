Kraken Parent Payward Brings 200 UK and EU Stocks Onchain as xStocks

Web3 · Decrypt · 2026-11-11T12:46:24.000Z

Kraken's parent company, Payward, has launched xStocks, tokenizing over 200 UK and EU stocks onto the blockchain, bringing its total tokenized assets past 1,500. While the new UK tokens are available, investors based in the UK cannot currently purchase them.
