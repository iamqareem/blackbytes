Just announced at electronica: the Arduino® Nano™ family is now XL!

IoT & Open Source · Arduino Blog · 2026-11-10T14:46:08.000Z

Arduino announced the Nano XL family at electronica 2026 in Munich, introducing two new boards designed for industrial, home, and building automation. The boards expand the Nano's compact form factor with on-board fieldbus transceivers and a DIN-rail-mountable carrier for professional deployment.
