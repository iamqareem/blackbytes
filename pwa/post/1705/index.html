South Africa's ANC party on track to face 'nightmare' losses in municipal elections

News · France24 Africa · 2026-11-06T07:15:07.000Z

South Africa's African National Congress party could face its worst-ever election result on Friday with about a third of support after 80 percent of the votes from local government polls counted. Final results are expected by Sunday.
