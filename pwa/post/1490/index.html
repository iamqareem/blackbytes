Whereabouts of Brazil's defeated presidential candidate Bolsonaro unknown

News · Africanews · 2026-10-28T11:12:32.000Z

The whereabouts of Brazil's defeated presidential candidate Flavio Bolsonaro were unknown on Wednesday after reports that he fled the country. Bolsonaro lost the runoff election to incumbent Luis Inacio Lula da Silva on Sunday but has refused to concede defeat.
