Haiti's ex-president Martelly registers as candidate for upcoming election

News · Africanews · 2026-10-24T14:09:35.000Z

Haiti’s former president Michel Martelly is running for office again. He presented his candidacy on Friday for the December 13 elections - the country’s first in 10 years. Earlier this month, Haiti’s electoral council overturned a ban on candidates sanctioned by foreign countries.
