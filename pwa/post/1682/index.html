Gates Foundation says Nigeria must build AI around real-world problems

Tech · TechCabal · 2026-10-28T14:59:04.000Z

Uche Amaonwu, Director of the Gates Foundation Nigeria Country Office, urged Lagos to focus AI on healthcare, education, agriculture and public services, announcing a $1 billion two‑year commitment to the Nigeria AI Building Hub at Lagos Business School.
