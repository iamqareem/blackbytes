Evolving metal extraction processes

AI & Quantum · MIT Technology Review · 2026-11-10T13:00:00.000Z

Outokumpu’s EvoMaterials platform reimagines metal extraction using novel chemical pathways to recover critical materials like chromium with lower emissions and higher efficiency. Development began in the US for Finnish chromite, with pilot-scale validation starting in New Hampshire in early 2027.
