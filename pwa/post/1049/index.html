Tigray is still at war, rebel leader warns, claiming tactical victories

News · Africanews · 2026-10-23T13:37:38.000Z

The leader of Ethiopia’s rebel Tigray People’s Liberation Front says the northern state is still at war. In a message aired on Tigrayan television on Wednesday, Debretsion Gebremichael said the TPLF is continuing to fight federal forces in the region and will “remain a graveyard for its enemies.”
