Zimbabwe’s Pizza Inn owner buys out Kenya delivery arm for $664,000

Tech · TechCabal · 2026-11-11T13:31:00.000Z

Simbisa Brands acquired the remaining 25% of Kutuma Kenya for $664,000, gaining full ownership of the delivery arm serving its fast-food chains and other merchants in Kenya.
