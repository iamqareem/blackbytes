Duolingo, Strava, and Ticketmaster Are All Caught Up in EU’s New Digital Fairness Rules

Tech · Wired · 2026-11-11T13:36:15.000Z

The EU's Digital Fairness Act targets major platforms like Duolingo, Strava, and Ticketmaster, mandating the disabling of "dark patterns" such as winning streaks and requiring influencers to disclose paid promotions.
