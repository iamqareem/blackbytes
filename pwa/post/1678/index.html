Egypt fines prominent editor Lina Attalah and shuts independent news outlet Mada Masr

News · The Guardian Africa · 2026-10-28T14:16:24.000Z

Cairo court fined Lina Attalah, editor‑in‑chief of Mada Masr, and ordered the outlet’s website shut down after convicting her of running an unlicensed site. The Egyptian authorities released her from detention amid a broader crackdown on independent media.
