Migrant boat sinks off Senegal, killing at least 34 people, authorities say

News · France24 Africa · 2026-11-08T20:40:33.000Z

A boat carrying migrants along the hazardous Atlantic Ocean route from West Africa to Europe sank off the coast of Senegal, killed at least 34 people, authorities said Sunday. Only one survivor has so far been found, and "an indeterminate number" are still missing.
