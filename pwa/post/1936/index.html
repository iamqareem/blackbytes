From Lagos to Cotonou! BN’s Osas is Taking You Inside SIMA 2026

Entertainment · BellaNaija · 2026-11-11T11:00:04.000Z

BellaNaija’s Osas Okonyon is traveling from Lagos to Cotonou, Republic of Benin, to cover the third edition of SIMA 2026, the Salon des Industries Musicales d’Afrique Francophone. The event convenes African music industry stakeholders to discuss the future of the continent’s sound and its global influence.
