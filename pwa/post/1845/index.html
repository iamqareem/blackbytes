Why lower transfer fees cannot easily break M-PESA’s mobile money dominance

Tech · TechCabal · 2026-11-10T15:56:51.000Z

PesaLink, Kenya's interbank payments network, is struggling to displace M-PESA's dominance despite expanding to 80 financial institutions and offering lower fees for large transfers, as M-PESA's extensive agent network and convenience for everyday transactions keep it preferred by consumers.
