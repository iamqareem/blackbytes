Midea Introduces Space Master Refrigerator in Nigeria, Bringing More Room to Everyday Living

Entertainment · BellaNaija · 2026-11-10T10:30:06.000Z

Midea has launched the Space Master Refrigerator in Nigeria, featuring a "Same Size. More Space" design to address household storage challenges. The appliance aims to provide increased capacity for groceries and meal prep, with consumers able to experience the product through in-store demonstrations at authorized outlets.
