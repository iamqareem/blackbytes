The Johannesburg Institute for Advanced Study (JIAS) Writing Fellowships 2027 for emerging African creatives (Fully Funded to Johannesburg, South Africa)

Education · Opportunities for Africans · 2026-11-09T04:57:00.000Z

The Johannesburg Institute for Advanced Study (JIAS) offers fully funded 2027 Writing Fellowships for emerging African creatives, academics, and researchers. The two-month program takes place in Johannesburg, South Africa, with an application deadline of November 30, 2026.
