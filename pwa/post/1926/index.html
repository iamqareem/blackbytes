Retrial of Egyptian journalist Lina Attalah adjourned amid criticism of media crackdown

Geopolitics · Middle East Eye · 2026-11-11T13:44:47.000Z

An Egyptian court adjourned the retrial of Mada Masr editor Lina Attalah until November 18, amid a crackdown on independent media. The proceedings, which include charges of publishing false news and operating without a licence, come as rights groups and US politicians condemn the government's restrictions on press freedom.
