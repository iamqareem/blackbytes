Nigeria’s active mobile subscriptions hit 200 million after NIN-SIM purge

Tech · TechCabal · 2026-11-11T13:11:15.000Z

Nigeria's active mobile subscriptions rebounded to 200.3 million in September 2026, recovering from a 2024 NIN-SIM purge that dropped figures to 164.9 million. The growth is concentrated among MTN and Airtel, which together hold 86% of the market.
