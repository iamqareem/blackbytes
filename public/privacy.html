Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected

  • Session ID: Anonymous identifier stored in localStorage to personalize your feed
  • Interaction Data: Views, likes, shares, saves, reactions on content (linked to session ID)
  • Preferences: Favorite categories you select (stored locally and on server)
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device info, referrer (standard server logs)

Cookies & Local Storage

  • Essential: Session ID, theme preference, cookie consent choice
  • Analytics: Interaction events for feed personalization (with consent)
  • Third-party: Google Analytics (GA4), Google AdSense (if enabled, with consent)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Personalize your content feed based on engagement and preferences
  • Improve content recommendations through heuristic ranking
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve the Service
  • Serve relevant advertisements (if enabled and consented)
  • Ensure security and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, AI summarization (Google Gemini)
  • Advertising Partners: Google AdSense (only with explicit consent)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights

4. Data Retention

  • Interaction data: 90 days (aggregated analytics retained longer)
  • Session preferences: Until you clear browser data or 1 year of inactivity
  • Server logs: 30 days

5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object: Opt-out of processing for analytics/ads
  • Withdraw Consent: At any time via cookie settings

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@gossip.ugaplex.com.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini: AI content summarization (content sent for processing)
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Aggregate usage analytics
  • Google AdSense: Advertising (only with consent)
  • Social Platforms: Content fetched from public TikTok, X/Twitter, YouTube, RSS feeds

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, contact us immediately.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption, secure cookies, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States (Google services). We ensure appropriate safeguards per applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be notified via the Service. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions? Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@gossip.ugaplex.com.
